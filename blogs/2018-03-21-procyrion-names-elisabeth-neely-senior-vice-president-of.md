Procyrion announces the appointment of Elisabeth (Beth) Neely, RAC, as Senior Vice President of Clinical and Regulatory Affairs. Neely brings to Procyrion more than 20 years of experience as a global regulatory affairs leader, having played a key role in the approval of neuromodulation and cardiac devices.
