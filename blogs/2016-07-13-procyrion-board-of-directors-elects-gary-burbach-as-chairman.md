Procyrion, the Houston-based developer of Aortix™, a percutaneous circulatory assist device intended to treat chronic heart failure (HF) and related conditions, today announced Gary Burbach as the company’s Chairman of the Board of Directors. Burbach previously served as an independent director for Procyrion.
