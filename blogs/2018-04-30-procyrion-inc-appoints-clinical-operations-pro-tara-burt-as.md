Procyrion, Inc. announced today the appointment of Tara Burt as Associate Director Clinical Affairs. Burt’s knowledge of clinical strategy, trial execution and operations will be a commanding asset to Procyrion as it continues to advance its intra-aortic heart pump for use in NYHA Class III-IVa heart failure patients
