Procyrion Inc. today announced they have won the People’s Choice Award from the American Heart Association’s (AHA) during the 2017 Health Tech Competition with their ground-breaking technology, Aortix™. The competition recognizes innovative healthcare solutions that improve patient outcomes for heart health.
