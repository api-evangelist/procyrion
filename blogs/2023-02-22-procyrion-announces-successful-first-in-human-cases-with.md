Procyrion announces successful first-in-human cases with Aortix™ pump to prevent acute kidney injury in patients undergoing cardiac surgery. Study adds to the growing body of data for the Aortix™ pump, which will be featured in a presentation at the Technology and Heart Failure Therapeutics conference in March.
