Heart pump developer Procyrion, Inc. has been selected to participate in the 2017 EuroPCR, which will take place May 16th to 19th at the Palais des Congrès in Paris, France. Chief Scientific Officer Jace Heuring will deliver a presentation and discuss Aortix™.
