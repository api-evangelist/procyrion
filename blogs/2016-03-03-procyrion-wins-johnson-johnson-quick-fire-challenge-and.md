Procyrion Inc. today announced they have won the Johnson & Johnson Quick Fire Challenge with their ground breaking technology, Aortix™, a catheter-based circulatory support device. Procyrion has been awarded a year residency in the new JLABS facility located within the Texas Medical Center’s Innovation Institute.
